datasets. Both were released by Renewable Energy Systems on behalf of The
Renewables Infrastructure Group under CC BY 4.0.

Clerc, A. (2026). *Altahullion wind farm open dataset*, version 1. Zenodo.
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19948235

## Hill of Towie wind farm

Clerc, A., and Lingkan, E. (2026). *Hill of Towie wind farm open dataset*,

The source records and their licence terms control reuse of the original
